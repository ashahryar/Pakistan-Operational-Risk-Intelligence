# Pakistan Operational Risk Intelligence — final system status (Task 39)

A reviewer should be able to understand the platform from this page without reading the source. Every number below comes from a committed audit file, the database, or a test; limitations are stated on purpose.

## Problem

Pakistan's flood and disaster information is published as scattered PDF and HTML bulletins from several agencies, with inconsistent place names and no shared geography. This platform collects those bulletins, turns them into validated tables on one canonical administrative geography, and serves **operational risk information that says how much evidence stands behind it**. Where evidence is insufficient the platform abstains instead of guessing.

## Data sources

| Source | Content | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| NDMA | situation reports: casualties, damage, rescue, relief | province level |
| PDMA Punjab | daily rainfall, river gauges and flows, daily sitreps | district / station level |
| PMD (and FFD) | city forecasts, weather alerts, weekly outlook, flood bulletins | one dated weather observation in the project today |
| WAPDA, Punjab Irrigation Department (FRAU) | owner statements and a headworks coordinate table, used as gauge-geography evidence only | see Geography |
| EPA Punjab | air quality | Lahore only |
| Document corpus | NDMA/PDMA/FFC reports and advisories | RAG evidence, never a numeric input |

## Pipeline

```
sources → extraction → raw files (data/raw) → parsing (golden-file tested) → validation / quarantine (dq.quarantine)
        → PostgreSQL 15 → Gold datasets → risk engine → risk.operational_risk
        → FastAPI (read-only) → Streamlit dashboard
                          ↘ RAG / intelligence / agent (read-only, evidence-grounded)
```

Orchestration is seven Airflow DAGs, all **paused** by design (the project runs the stages by hand; DAG integrity is tested). AWS is limited to S3 upload code; Glue, Redshift and Lambda are inactive and nothing paid is deployed. PostGIS and pgvector are not installed: boundaries are processed in pure Python and embeddings sit in an ordinary table.

## Geography

* **Canonical geography** (`geo.admin_unit`): 1 country, 7 provinces, 69 districts. IDs never change.
* **Evidence-backed aliases**: a boundary-to-canonical name match is added only when an official source states the equivalence (for example Sindh Act XIV of 2023 for Nawabshah / Shaheed Benazirabad). Spelling similarity is never used. Registry: `config/crosswalk_evidence.yaml`.
* **Crosswalk to the COD-AB boundary file** (a third-party humanitarian compilation, not government-certified, vintage 2022): 54 exact, 3 alias, 103 unmatched districts. Unmatched means the canonical model has no such unit; the model was not extended.
* **Gauge stations** (41 stations, 3,686 observations): 2 stations have an eligible mapping — **Chashma → Mianwali** (WAPDA states it) and **Trimmu → Jhang** (Punjab Irrigation Department coordinates that stay in one district over their stated offset). The other 39 are unresolved, conflicting or secondary-only. Registry: `config/gauge_station_evidence.yaml`.
* **Why unsupported mappings are not applied**: Tarbela is stated to be in Swabi by WAPDA, but Swabi is not in the canonical model and secondary sources also name Haripur; Rasul is conflicting (Jhelum / Mandi Bahauddin); Marala, Khanki and Balloki rest on secondary sources only; Mangla's seed row is not a real district; 23 hill-torrent and nullah sites have no evidence. Conflicts are never resolved by majority vote.

## Risk: what is produced, what is scored, what abstains

`risk.operational_risk` holds **1,771 rows** over 62 areas (2025-03-03 → 2026-09-16), generated by the deterministic engine `risk-engine-1.0.0`:

| Status | Rows |
|---|---:|
| INSUFFICIENT_DATA | 1,280 |
| LOW | 385 |
| MODERATE | 48 |
| HIGH | 36 |
| CRITICAL | 22 |

These statuses compare observed signals with **PROVISIONAL** thresholds that are not validated against outcomes. They are not probabilities.

**`risk_score` is NULL on every row, and `score_v2` is ABSTAINED everywhere. This is intentional.** The measured reasons (`data/analytics/risk/score_v2_audit.json`):

1. **No evidence-based weights exist** and there are no labelled outcomes. Combining signals would need weights; inventing them (for example rain 30 %, flood 40 %) would be fabrication. The shipped config has `enabled: false` and `weights: {}`.
2. **The data contract is rarely met.** A score needs at least two independent eligible signal groups (hydromet event, temperature, air quality) for the same area and date, each with at least 30 prior observations. Of 1,771 cells, 1,287 have no eligible group, 476 have one, and **8 have two**.
3. Status thresholds are provisional.

Eligible observations by domain: air quality 320 (Lahore), rainfall 46, gauge 126 (Mianwali and Jhang), weather 0 (one date). Hazard alerts and disaster events are contextual and never numeric.

**Integration of the Task 38 gauge evidence.** The engine consumes only eligible station mappings. The stored risk table had been generated before the two mappings existed, so the audit counted 126 eligible gauge observations that production ignored. Task 39 regenerated the risk output (185 new rows for Mianwali and Jhang, no duplicates, no row removed) and loaded it with the non-destructive upsert; the audit now has 1,771 cells, equal to the table. Gauge evidence alone never yields a score: no unit has two independent groups from it.

**What a consumer sees.** Every risk response carries `score_v2`: `score_status`, `abstention_reason`, `evidence_available`, `evidence_missing`, `evidence_required`, and an `interpretation` that states a null score does not mean low risk. The dashboard shows "scored" and "abstained" counts, shows null as "unavailable" (never 0), and never turns missing evidence into a low or green state.

**What broader scoring needs:** outcome labels or validated reference thresholds; evidence-referenced weights per signal group; more than one geography with continuous data (only Lahore air quality qualifies); resolved gauge geography; weather history of at least 31 dated observations; a second year of data for seasonality.

## RAG, intelligence and agent

Documents are chunked and embedded (1,909 embeddings); retrieval is hybrid (keyword + semantic) with a relevance and abstention policy that returns `NO_EVIDENCE` for off-topic queries. The intelligence endpoint and the read-only agent answer only from allowlisted tools and label every fact `RISK_ENGINE`, `RAG_DOCUMENT`, `ML_MODEL`, `BASELINE_MODEL` or `GEOGRAPHY`. They cannot change geography or scores and abstain when evidence is insufficient. No real language model was available during development, so answer quality of a real model is not measured; the validators and abstention paths are tested with a scripted provider.

## Machine learning

Task 33 provides **baseline** forecasts (228 stored predictions) for gauge discharge at qualifying stations. They are experimental: they are compared with a persistence baseline, are labelled `BASELINE_ONLY`, and never feed a risk score or a status. No accuracy claim is made beyond the stored evaluation metrics.

## Serving

* **API** (FastAPI, read-only; write verbs answer 405): health, risk (list, latest, map, by unit), geography, weather, disasters, ML predictions, RAG, intelligence, agent.
* **Dashboard** (Streamlit): the original pages (NDMA casualties and damage, PMD weather, PDMA rainfall and rivers) plus the risk map, RAG, intelligence and agent pages.

## Limitations (stated on purpose)

* 39 of 41 gauge stations have no authoritative geography; the only official coordinates found are breaching-section locations, not gauge sites.
* Weather has a single dated observation, so it cannot be normalized; air-quality data is continuous only for Lahore.
* There are no outcome labels and no evidence-based weights, hence no numeric score.
* Canonical geography has 69 districts; 103 boundary districts (including Swabi and Haripur) are not in it.
* FFC and IRSA were unreachable during development (HTTP 500 and refused connection), so absence of evidence there is not proof it does not exist.
* Risk statuses use provisional thresholds; rows with HIGH or CRITICAL for Mianwali and Jhang come from the two gauge mappings and share that caveat.
* The two gauge mappings assume the PDMA station is the owner's same-named site on the same river; both are marked `pending_manual_review`.
* The data is a snapshot (June–September 2026 for gauges); the Airflow DAGs are paused and have not been run unattended.
